# BeeBook – Beekeeping Journal Support

Welcome to the official support page for BeeBook.

BeeBook is a beekeeping management and journal application designed to help beekeepers organize their apiaries, hives, inspections, production, tasks and records.

## Support

If you experience a problem with BeeBook, have a question, or would like to suggest an improvement, please contact us.

Email: v.todorow93@gmail.com

## BeeBook Features

- Apiary and hive management
- Hive inspection journal
- Calendar and reminders
- Honey and bee product tracking
- Apiary statistics
- Production and inventory management
- Local backup and restore

## Privacy

BeeBook is designed to keep the beekeeper's records under the user's control.

A separate Privacy Policy is available for the application.
